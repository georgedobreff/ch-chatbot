# Companies House fees

*Source: https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/companies-house-fees/companies-house-fees*

These registration fees do not apply to documents delivered under the Companies Act 1985 or the Companies (Northern Ireland) Order 1986. Any document delivered under these acts will be free of charge.

We charge a statutory filing fee for:

- company incorporation and registration
- limited liability partnership (LLP) incorporation and registration fees
- overseas company incorporation and registration fees
- community interest company (CIC) and ‘other’ incorporation types
- limited partnership (LP) incorporation fees
- our extractives filing service

For our services, we also charge:

- Find and update company information fees
- contact centre search fees
- XML gateway search fees
- bulk product fees
Some of our fees are set by regulations and confirmed by parliament. Some fees are administratively set in our fees determination.
