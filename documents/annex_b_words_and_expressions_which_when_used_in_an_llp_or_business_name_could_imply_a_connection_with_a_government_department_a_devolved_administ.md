# Limited liability partnership incorporation and names

*Source: https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/limited-liability-partnership-incorporation-and-names/annex-b-words-and-expressions-which-when-used-in-an-llp-or-business-name-could-imply-a-connection-with-a-government-department-a-devolved-administ*

## 1. Accounting Council / Actuarial Council / Audit and Assurance Council / Corporate Reporting Council

To use these expressions or anything similar in your proposed name, please provide a letter or email of non-objection from the Financial Reporting Council.

[enquiries@frc.org.uk](mailto:enquiries@frc.org.uk)

Financial Reporting Council8th Floor125 London WallLondonEC2Y 5AS

## 2. Agency

You can use this word in your proposed name provided it does not imply a connection with a government department or body, a devolved administration or a local or specified public authority.

## 3. Assembly

You can use this word in your proposed name provided it does not imply a connection with a government department or body, a devolved administration or a local or specified public authority.

## 4. Border Agency / Border Force

The use of these expressions in a proposed name could imply a connection with the UK Border Force or UK Visas and Immigration, (both part of the Home Office).

If you proceed with your application, please include a copy of a letter or email of non-objection from the Home Office.

[public.enquiries@homeoffice.gsi.gov.uk](mailto:public.enquiries@homeoffice.gsi.gov.uk)

Home OfficeDirect Communications Unit2 Marsham StreetLondonSW1P 4DF

## 15. Industrial and Provident Society

Industrial and Provident Societies (now known as Registered Societies in England, Wales and Scotland) are registered and regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority, or in Northern Ireland by the Department for the Economy.

You cannot use this expression in a LLP or business name. For further information, please contact the relevant body:

[consumer.queries@fca.org.uk](mailto:consumer.queries@fca.org.uk)

Financial Conduct Authority25 The North ColonnadeCanary WharfLondonE14 5HS

[registry.info@economy-ni.gov.uk](mailto:registry.info@economy-ni.gov.uk)

Registry of Credit Unions and Industrial & Provident SocietiesDepartment for the EconomyAdelaide House39-49 Adelaide StreetBELFASTBT2 8FD

## 25. SE

To use this expression in this form (upper or lower case) at the start or end of your proposed name, you should be a ‘Societas Europaea’ (European Company) as specified in European Council Regulation (EC) No 2157/200 (October 2001) and European Public Limited Liability Company Regulations 2004 (No. 2326).

## 26. Secondary Education

You can use this expression in your proposed name provided it does not imply a connection with education services provided by a local authority, a government department or a devolved administration.  If the name is likely to imply a connection, please provide a letter or email of non-objection from a relevant body.
