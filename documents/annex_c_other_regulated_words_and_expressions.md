# Limited liability partnership incorporation and names

*Source: https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/limited-liability-partnership-incorporation-and-names/annex-c-other-regulated-words-and-expressions*

The use of any of these words and expressions in a LLP or business name could be a criminal offence. The list is not exhaustive and applicants should carry out their own checks to ensure their proposed name is lawful.

## 1. Anzac

[The “Anzac” (Restriction on Trade Use of Word) Act 1916](http://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/Geo5/6-7/51/section/1) makes it an offence to use this word or anything similar in connection with any trade, business or profession.

## 2. Architect

To use this word in your proposed LLP or business name, please provide an email or letter confirming you are authorised to use this title from the Architects Registration Board (ARB).

[info@arb.org.uk](mailto:info@arb.org.uk)

Architects Registration Board8 Weymouth StreetLondonW1W 5BU

Names that include ‘naval architect’, ‘landscape architect’ or ‘golf-course architect’ are not restricted.

## 3. Bachelor of medicine

This title is protected by The Medical Act 1983. To support your application to use this expression in a LLP or business name, please provide a copy of your practising certificate or evidence of your inclusion on the list of Registered Medical Practitioners.

## 4. Building Society

Building Societies are regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority (FCA) and the Prudential Regulation Authority. This expression cannot be used in a LLP or business name.

For further information, please contact the FCA.

[consumer.queries@fca.org.uk](mailto:consumer.queries@fca.org.uk)

Sensitive Business Names TeamFinancial Conduct Authority25 North Colonnade Canary WharfLondonE14 5HS

## 7. Citius / Altius / Fortius

This expression, its plurals, translations and anything similar, is protected by The Olympic Symbol etc. (Protection) Act 1995. Its use in a LLP or business name could commit an offence. If you decide to proceed with your application, please seek the views of the British Olympic Association and include a copy of their response.

## 8. Commonhold Association

This expression can only be used in the name of a limited by guarantee LLP registered as a Commonhold Association in accordance with the Commonhold Regulations 2004 (SI No.1829) as amended by The Commonhold (Amendment) Regulations 2009 (SI No. 2363).

## 9. Contact lens / Contact lenses

If your proposed LLP or business intends to fit contact lenses, please provide evidence that you are a registered medical practitioner, optometrist or dispensing optician authorised to carry out this activity.

If your proposed LLP or business intends to manufacture or supply contact lenses (but will not fit lenses) please confirm this with your application.
